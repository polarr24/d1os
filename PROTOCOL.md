## RB ball security

Coach Diaz’s correction drives Today, the drill list, error taxonomy, film questions, post-game review and the next practice cue. A logged error and coach note become the current correction. Quality is reported as errors/observed reps, with denominator and drill shown, not as an official game-fumble rate.

1. Five-point audit: 2 rounds × 5 checks each arm (20 checks). Fingers/tip, palm/underside, forearm, elbow pocket/rear tip, ribs/chest. High and tight; wrist firm and elbow connected. Coaches count points differently; have Diaz check the actual grip.
2. Two-hand traffic walk: 2 × 5 entries. Second hand arrives before traffic. Do not switch the ball amid defenders.
3. Sideline/finish reset: 2 × 4 walkthrough finishes. See the boundary, protect possession and restore pressure after simulated contact. Do not reach the ball into traffic.
4. Controlled bag lane: 2 × 4 passes, **coach-led only**. No extra collision session.
5. Gentle predictable ball resistance: 2 × 3 holds per arm, **coach-led only**. Pressure to ball only; no joint twisting or blows.
6. Ground-contact protection: 2 × 3 low-position reps, **coach-led on suitable mats**. No diving, partner throws or hard-ground falling. Do not post a locked arm or continue an arm fight after the whistle.
7. Fatigue carry audit: 1 × 5 checks per arm after normal practice only if coach approves. No added gassers to manufacture fatigue.

Doses are coaching heuristics [DESIGN]. USA Football’s graduated-contact framework supports coach-led progression, not a quantified benefit from these exact reps [CONTACT]. Saved actual reps indicate completion; planned reps do not. Weekly views separate drills and remind the athlete to compare similar setting/supervision. Error types include exposed tip, elbow separation, low carry, loose wrist, late second hand, unsafe switch, arm fight, ground loss, unknown-ruling loss and a separately confirmed official fumble.

## Safety

Red flags override every training score. Suspected concussion after a head/body impact: remove from sport, tell parent/coach/trainer, obtain healthcare assessment, no same-day return [CONCUSSION]. App holds cannot be cleared the same day. Later professional authorization is recorded as an athlete report, not generated by the app. Future-dated follow-up cannot clear a present hold.

Severe/worsening headache, confusion, neurological symptoms, fainting/collapse, chest pain, severe unexplained breathlessness, suspected heat stroke or serious neck injury need urgent adult/professional help; call 911 for severe or worsening signs. Avoid moving someone with suspected serious neck injury. Joint swelling, recurring focal bone/joint/tendon pain, altered gait, movement-changing pain, worsening night pain and other suspected injury stop dependent training and require trainer/clinician review. No completion reward or workaround for pushing through.
