# Decision rules and limits

D1OS records observations and organizes coaching decisions. It is not a clinical service.

An active symptom overrides app workouts even with good readiness or a scheduled game. A shin episode cannot be cleared by a generic symptom-resolution entry, a lower pain score, elapsed days, or an Ascend habit. Modified or graded-return directions stay as records; the app does not invent or run that rehabilitation program.

A shin-specific unrestricted-return entry must name a clinician or athletic trainer, include actual directions, the actual assessment date, and explicit confirmation. It applies only to its linked episode. A later symptom report reopens the hold. These entries are self-reported and not independently verified. A same-day assessment may be recorded truthfully, without changing its date. The app's no-same-day reopening rule is a conservative implementation choice, not a medical recovery threshold.

Injury-history changes invalidate the prior development-context review. A fresh coached block is required so time away is not followed by an automatic jump to a later training week. Readiness, symptom, supervision, load, rest, schedule and team-sport conditions still apply after a reported return. A software form cannot grant medical clearance.

While a symptom hold is active, physical prescription screens show the hold workflow, and calorie/bulk heuristics are suppressed. Historical observations remain in local storage and exports. There is no injury-based weight-cut rule, hormone target, missed-session debt or penalty for recovery days.

Other app cutoffs—including 48-hour recent-load holds, coach-review expiry, and uncertainty bands supplied by users—are transparent software/coaching assumptions. They are not diagnostic thresholds, measured fatigue, injury probabilities, or optimal individual training prescriptions.

Clinical education used for the shin page was checked October 7, 2026: AAP's Shin Pain page, Children's Hospital Colorado's Shin Pain and Shin Splints page, and AAOS's Compartment Syndrome page. They support seeking assessment and distinguishing overlapping possible causes; they do not validate this software or the exact workflow. Source links are available on the recovery page.
