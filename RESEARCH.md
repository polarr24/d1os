# D1OS 14.1 — research and numerical-prescription ledger

Research checked September 25–26, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. The four audit additions were checked September 26; original retrieval dates are retained below. “Retrieved” means the stated page, abstract or indexed text was accessible—not that every cited underlying study was independently audited. This is a targeted implementation review, not a registered systematic review.

## Evidence rules

The athlete’s current brief controls personal facts. External publications inform general principles, not personal clearance or a verified performance rank. Official clinical guidance takes precedence for safety. Reviews and trials retain their population limits. Exact app doses are labeled DESIGN when chosen by the builder.

No age-, maturation-, test-method- and population-matched normative dataset was established for Zayden. Therefore there is no ten-yard percentile table, D1 threshold or automatic “easy lift” max estimate.

## Corrections and independent judgment

- **Maintenance evidence is indirect.** An adult professional-soccer intervention cannot prove that this plan maintains all of a 15-year-old RB/MLB’s strength. It supports evaluating lower volume, while longitudinal performance and recovery guide changes.
- **Concurrent training is not inherently incompatible.** Youth evidence does not justify a simplistic “football kills strength” rule. Actual combined load, sequencing, maturity and recovery are the relevant constraints.
- **Taper windows are not universal physiology.** The app’s 48/72/96-hour rules are transparent, conservative decisions around the reported schedule.
- **Tired running is not maximal-speed practice.** Conditioning and game exposure have separate labels; neither is silently treated as a measured sprint dose.
- **Timing methods are not exchangeable.** No hand-to-electronic conversion or fabricated ten-yard percentile is supplied.
- **15 g/day creatine appeared in older drafting.** That does not establish actual current intake, adolescent maintenance dosing or medical approval. The new product log requires the real label and amount.
- **No source-grade inflation.** Narrative reviews, hospital summaries, coaching frameworks and inaccessible full-text records retain those labels. A recent publication date alone is not “Grade A” evidence.
- **No mechanistic guarantees.** The Atlas explains likely movement relationships, not inevitable speed gains or injury prevention.
- **No fake calorie measurement.** Bodyweight trend is observed/reported; total daily expenditure remains estimated. Fluid and supplement changes can confound short trends.
- **No current clearance from a historical symptom report.** A new symptom always overrides an old reassuring entry.

## Deliberately unresolved evidence questions

No controlled evidence retrieved establishes the exact optimal Thursday-game training week for a 15-year-old, two-way freshman with this school’s practice content. Sprint tolerance, maturation, technique, contact dose and nutrition needs remain individual. The app therefore exposes conservative decisions and missing information instead of fabricating precision.

Some primary-paper pages returned access challenges. Their indexed abstracts were used only for claims visible there; a full-text audit is not claimed. No external publication independently verifies Zayden’s measurements, school eligibility, game schedule, statistics or coach statements.


## v14.1 audit corrections

- All recent work matters: outside activity and low-quality sets now participate in the decision. The app does not claim to quantify total physiological fatigue.
- General AAP resistance-training recommendations do not prove the Saturday/Sunday split. Familiarity, progressive warm-up and observed technique are now required before working sets.
- Recurrent localized pain can deserve assessment before it changes movement; the check-in no longer waits for altered mechanics.
- Adequate energy supports growth even on rest days. A flat scale trend does not justify automatic calorie escalation or diagnosis.
- The NCAA reference is current guidance with a counselor pathway; there is no automated transcript audit or recruiting prediction.
- Detailed rationale and unresolved development gaps are recorded in AUDIT.md.
