# Real remaining gaps — D1OS 14.1.1

| Missing | Why | User impact | What closes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical iPhone 17 / iOS Safari verification | Available browser test runtime is Chromium/Linux, not an iOS device or simulator | Real keyboard, safe areas, standalone install, download flow and suspension may differ | Test the hosted bundle on an iPhone; check airplane-mode reload, logging, keyboard, exports and larger type |
| Coach review of exact lifting/sprint doses and equipment | No live coach review, movement assessment or equipment inventory supplied | Suggestions remain conditional on daily checks and actual warm-up/familiarity confirmation; athlete-entered coach records are not independently verified | Coach confirms exercises, supervision, training age, bar/plates, sprint inclusion and recent load |
| Actual fatigue physiology | RPE, sleep and soreness are self-report; no validated individual recovery model | App can suppress inappropriate extra work but cannot predict injury or establish readiness | Repeated athlete data plus coach/trainer judgment; do not add a fake readiness precision score |
| Verified performance baseline | No standardized sprint/jump measurements; lifts lack protocol/reps | No honest percentile, verified max or baseline progress claim | Repeat tests with recorded method/surface/start/device; coach observes lift standards |
| Film and official Durango statistics | Athlete unsure footage exists; exact date/yards/longest/fumble ruling not supplied | Review uses memory; unknown numbers stay unknown | Coach-provided footage/stat sheet; record sources and dated corrections |
| Basic defensive language / opponent scouting | Playbook, calls and actual opponent film are not supplied | Generic concepts cannot assign gaps, coverage or keys | Coach enters assigned terminology and scout observations |
| Nutrition calibration, growth context and complete intake | No three-week weigh-in/intake series, maturation context, lunch schedule or food access inventory; recovery concern flags are not diagnostic | Starting ranges are heuristic, not measured energy needs; game meal windows need school fit | Log representative data; parent/sports dietitian reviews appetite, growth, food access and recovery |
| Supplement labels and professional review | Exact current products/amounts and review documents are unknown | Flags remain; no automatic dose or certification verification | Record labels/lot, actual amounts, parent awareness and clinician directions |
| Historical-data semantic coverage | Actual attachment has code/seed state, not the athlete’s browser database or full prior backup | Unmapped legacy fields survive in archives but may not power new charts | Import the real export and review its migration preview; add tested mappings only for known fields |
| Cross-device sync and background reminders | Deliberately local-only, no account or server; browser suspension limits timers | Data does not appear automatically on another device; no background alarm guarantee | Manual JSON export/import now; a future explicitly authorized encrypted sync/notification design |
| Academics/recruiting and other-season plans | Grades, courses, eligibility records, recruiting contacts, wrestling/track calendars unknown | Path now has current NCAA guidance and structured school follow-up, but cannot audit a transcript or prescribe those seasons | Counselor/coach inputs and current official requirements; new season-specific evidence review |

The five largest product weaknesses remain device verification, heuristic workload decisions, missing observed baselines/film, uncalibrated nutrition, and manual backup/sync. These are visible limitations, not hidden completion claims.
