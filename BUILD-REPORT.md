## Screens changed or added

Today, Train and Path now connect daily execution, outside activity, supervised warm-up, working-load review, weekly review and college/school follow-up. Readiness adds illness, focal pain, appetite, stress and extra-load review. Runner records technique. Speed and Fuel explain the new decisions. Lab’s evidence ledger includes four additional official/primary sources. Field’s existing ball-security, MLB and film relationships remain intact.

## Major engine changes

All logged activity contributes to the recovery decision. Hard outside work, near-failure sets and deteriorating technique hold optional training. Actual activity timestamps matter. Logged maximal/high-speed work, prior lower lifting and outside activity hold extra maximal sprinting. Six consecutive observed training days protect a rest day.

Warm-up/familiarity confirmation precedes working sets. Progression is a coach review after repeatable complete sessions, not an automatic increase. Equipment limitations cannot be overridden by reduced readiness. Future-dated follow-up cannot clear a present symptom.

Nutrition shows actual complete-day intake alongside estimates, without an automatic increase from flat weight. Repeated recovery concerns prompt adult review without a diagnosis. Weekly focus appears on Today; coach briefs download locally.

School follow-up reflects freshman course planning for Class of 2030. Wrestling/track transitions remain explicit future planning needs. ## Research claims corrected

General youth training recommendations do not establish the exact weekend split. A reported lift does not establish movement competence. Focal overuse pain can matter before it impairs performance. Flat bodyweight is not measured energy expenditure. Repeated appetite/fatigue concerns do not diagnose REDs. The ledger has **21 external sport/health/eligibility sources plus DESIGN**, with additional Apple installation documentation. Abstract-only and later inaccessible full-text states remain labeled.

## Deliberately removed

Automatic calorie-target escalation, calendar-only permission for extra work, progression implications from incomplete/unknown-quality sets, duplicated sprint opportunities, equipment-mode override, and future-dated resolution of a current symptom. The misleading “power first” lower-day title was corrected. No new supplement dose, max-test program, punitive streak or extra conditioning was added.

## Five strongest improvements

1. Shared load decisions include outside sport and actual set quality.
2. Warm-up, movement familiarity and coach-reviewed loads precede progression.
3. Duplicate sprint doses and post-lifting sprint finishers are held.
4. Food/growth/recovery review replaces false precision from flat weight.
5. Daily execution, weekly evidence, coach questions and school follow-up form a usable review cycle.

## Five biggest remaining weaknesses

1. No observed movement assessment, standardized sprint baseline or confirmed game film.
2. Workload/readiness rules are heuristics, not measured fatigue or injury prediction.
3. Growth, food access, complete intake and professional supplement review remain unknown.
4. This is an in-season football system; other seasons/off-season require actual schedules and coach coordination.
5. Physical-iPhone validation and long-term storage remain unverified; backups are manual.

Other unknowns include exact lift methods, equipment, full practice/contact content, Basic’s defensive language, official Durango statistics/ruling, opponent film and academic transcript. None were filled with guesses.

## What I disagreed with from the previous build and why

The previous build improved the weekly template but did not sufficiently connect all actual work to training permission. It treated a nutrition heuristic as an automatic adjustment and lacked a meaningful progression/review loop. Those gaps could encourage more work or food-number chasing without establishing that either improves the athlete’s situation.

I kept the conservative in-season direction, but made its limits explicit. Saturday upper plus Sunday lower is a conditional option, not proven optimal physiology. Useful practice, observed football skill, recovery and school progress need to drive decisions. AUDIT.md gives the full rationale and unresolved development gaps.
