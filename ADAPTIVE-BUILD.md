# Adaptive Build — evidence and decision rules

## Scientific boundary

The app organizes familiar supervised training around measured field transfer, recoverability and repeatable working sets. It does not know maximum recoverable volume, pubertal stage, protein synthesis, fast-twitch proportion or an individual optimum. The goal is useful development—not the largest scale change.

Three-day full-body is the default. Four-day upper/lower distributes work with separated limb-region exposures; it is not supported here as superior adolescent programming. Eligibility is reported competence, not body size alone. Growth spurts, altered coordination, new equipment and injury require a fresh review; maturation is not inferred from photos.

## Evidence anchors

- [AAP, Stricker et al., 2020; reaffirmed November 2024](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/145/6/e20201011/76942/Resistance-Training-for-Children-and-Adolescents): youth resistance training is technique driven and supervised. Light initial work can progress from 1–2 sets toward 2–4 with demonstrated competence; generally 2–3 nonconsecutive training days.
- [Pelland et al., online December 2025 / journal February 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41343037/): 67 studies, 2,058 participants, mean age approximately 25 years. Dose-response models show diminishing returns and distinguish direct/indirect work. This does not establish an adolescent’s optimal set count or authorize unlimited volume.
- [Currier et al., 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10579494/): resistance prescriptions in healthy adults have outcome-specific strength/hypertrophy evidence. Adult muscle gain is not equivalent to improved teenage football performance.
- [Damas et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27219125/): investigated measured protein synthesis, damage and hypertrophy. Subjective soreness and acute damage signals are not app-accessible muscle-growth assays.
- [AAP shin-pain education](https://www.aap.org/globalassets/publications/coya/shin_pain_final_secured.1.0.pdf): multiple causes include bone stress injury and compartment syndrome. Being able to run through pain does not establish safety.

Focused training-design evidence review: October 9, 2026, not an exhaustive systematic review. The specific Togi video was not supplied or verified; a video anecdote is not treated as a controlled study. Existing compound cards retain their own earlier review dates.

## Twelve-week progression

| Weeks | Emphasis | Main / assistance sets | Main rep range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Familiarity, tolerance and repeatable baseline | 2 / 1 | 8–10 |
| 3 | Controlled muscle-building volume | 3 / 2 | 6–10 |
| 4 | Deload | 1 / 1 | 8–10 |
| 5–7 | Force with retained muscle work | 3 / 2 | 6–8 |
| 8 | Deload / transfer review | 1 / 1 | 8–10 |
| 9–11 | Speed/power expression with less compound fatigue | 2 / 2 | 6–8 |
| 12 | Consolidate and reassess | 1 / 1 | 8–10 |

The first three template movements use main prescriptions; later movements use assistance, generally 8–12 reps. Exact sets, phases and ranges are coaching/product starting hypotheses, not validated optimums. No heavier load is generated at a phase change; current familiar coach-selected load/repetitions are reported separately.

Established competence uses at least two reserve reps, foundation competence at least three, deload/review at least four. Reserve reps are subjective; coach observation matters. Falling below the current minimum or deteriorating technique ends the runner. Compound rest is normally 180 seconds, assistance 120. Short/recovery-adjusted sessions remove volume—not rest. Substitutions form a separate baseline.

## Exercise progression

Two latest exposures on different dates must contain every planned set exactly once, at the planned load, upper end of the prescribed rep range, steady technique and adequate reserve. Planned volume, equipment and actual load must match across exposures. Success opens a coach review—not an automatic weight increase. Otherwise the action is retain load/build reps or hold/reduce/review execution and recovery.

Prescriptions expire after fourteen days and at phase change. Session snapshots preserve what was prescribed at execution; later decisions do not rewrite them. Replacing a block cannot create a second workout after completed working sets that day.

## Recovery and speed

Unresolved symptoms, unknown/stale calendar, organized sport, overdue review, changed injury/sport context, recent hard work and red readiness hold extra work. Previous-day same-region lifting inside forty-eight hours holds a development exposure. Six preceding activity days protect a full rest day. These are conservative product rules, not measured healing times.

A follow-up in the previous twenty-four hours with soreness 7+/10 or energy 2/5 or lower holds work for review. Moderate soreness 4+/10 requests reduced volume and suppresses maximal speed/power. New/worsening injury pain creates a persistent symptom episode. Missing reports mean unknown—not recovered. Follow-up hours are measured from session-plan opening, not proven exercise completion.

Up to two approved speed exposures are embedded before lifting, not additional hard days. Familiar starts establish tolerance. Extending distance, established flying work, sled/hill substitutions and familiar low-volume power require actual coaching. Deload/review weeks, existing speed/lower exposure, other activity and inadequate readiness suppress maximal speed. The app does not derive loading, tendon stiffness, fiber type or force from physique.

## Four-exposure training pilot

This is feasibility/performance-tolerance monitoring, not a clinical trial or hypertrophy experiment.

1. Establish two weeks of tolerance and a current reviewed block. Choose a familiar movement already repeated twice weekly, with a fixed current load/repetition prescription.
2. Before outcomes, register the question, exact equipment, target load/reps, coach/parent review, confounds, stop rules and a descriptive reserve-rep threshold.
3. Randomize balanced ABBA or BAAB. Allowed variables: 120 vs 180 seconds between existing target sets, or target movement before/after adjacent familiar assistance. No condition adds work.
4. Compatible future runner sessions apply the assignment. A timer/checkbox cannot prove compliance; actual execution must be reviewed/reported.
5. Attach every matching completed source set. Endpoint: mean reported reserve reps at fixed load/reps—reported easier execution, not measured recovery, hypertrophy or athletic improvement.
6. Retain skips and deviations. Changed equipment/load/reps, incomplete sets, bad technique and low reserve are excluded. Stop when phase/context changes or recovery is held. Do not add sessions to finish.
7. Report condition means, their descriptive difference and the registered threshold. Source sets are rechecked. No p-value, causal treatment estimate, growth forecast or biological optimum is generated; no prescription changes automatically.

Four unblinded observations cannot settle an optimum. Puberty, learning, expectation, RIR accuracy, carry-over, order, food, sleep and other training confound results. Repeating weak pilots does not make them clinical evidence. PEDs, supplement/drug changes, fasting, sleep deprivation, maximal tests and failure experiments are not supported variables.

## Long-term growth and transfer

Require three distinct morning weights in each of three consecutive seven-day windows before displaying median-to-median change. This is a reporting threshold, not a demand to weigh daily. Water, glycogen, food, fat and normal growth affect bodyweight. No lean-mass or body-fat estimate is invented.

Review same-setup acceleration/flying tests, jumps, load/bodyweight, comfort and sport skill alongside mass. Bigger plus slower means review—not automatically eat more or cut. A parent and pediatric sports dietitian should review deliberate weight-change targets. Regular meals/snacks, carbohydrate for work, protein-containing meals, adequate fluids and sleep opportunity support development; crash bulk/cut experiments do not belong here.

The 24-month direction and multisport calendars remain, but the next reviewable block—not a rigid two-year bulk—governs execution. Coach selection depends on role, skill, team needs, performance and health; physique cannot guarantee varsity.
